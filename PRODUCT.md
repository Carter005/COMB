# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Public visitors, token participants, and on-chain researchers who want to observe and question a multi-agent AI system. A visitor should understand within seconds that the terminal shows eight distinct AI agents interpreting verifiable activity on Robinhood Chain.

## Product Purpose

THE OCTOPUS is an on-chain multi-agent AI composed of eight specialized agents with distinct responsibilities, temperaments, and voices. The agents observe Robinhood Chain, independently interpret market evidence, dispute one another, and preserve their conclusions as inspectable market memory.

The website is the live operating terminal for this system. Visitors can watch the agents work, ask O8 questions, inspect cited evidence, and follow the state of the `$O8` network.

## Canonical Story

O8 begins dormant. It has a chain connection, eight specialized arms, and no target identity. When an official `$O8` contract address created through Pons Launchpad is registered, the organism awakens, verifies its origin, and attaches every arm to the same contract.

Blocks become time. Transactions become signals. Wallets become observed participants. Liquidity changes become environmental pressure. Verified events become memory. A visitor question can invoke one AI voice, but it never changes the underlying evidence.

O8 does not claim consciousness or prediction. Its growth is the measurable accumulation of verified evidence and behavioral history around one contract.

## Lifecycle

The lifecycle is derived from real target state, never advanced by a decorative timer or random number.

1. `00 DORMANT` — no official CA is bound; the organism only samples the network environment.
2. `01 AWAKENED` — a candidate CA is being verified against Pons.
3. `02 ATTACHED` — origin is verified and all eight arms are bound to the target.
4. `03 FEEDING` — the Pons curve is active; O8 ingests transactions, wallets, liquidity and contract evidence.
5. `04 ASCENDING` — graduation is approaching; the arms focus on flow, liquidity and anomalies.
6. `05 GRADUATED` — migration into open liquidity is verified and preserved.
7. `06 ADAPTING` — current behavior can be compared with retained target history.
8. `07 ARCHIVED` — live observation has ended, but the evidence record remains inspectable.

## Narrative Rules

- “Food” means verifiable chain evidence, not fictional energy.
- “Memory” means persisted chain events, deterministic parser output, and clearly labeled on-request AI responses.
- “Adaptation” means comparison against retained historical baselines, not random mutation.
- “Emotion” changes an arm's voice and attention, never facts.
- Lifecycle transitions require a real target or explicit backend state.
- `CHAIN`, `RULE`, `AI`, and `VISUAL` remain visibly separated at all times.

## Lifecycle Memory

Each real transition creates one immutable lifecycle memory per target and phase. Repeated polling cannot manufacture duplicate awakenings, attachments, ascents, or graduations. Every record retains the target address and the available launch, pool, block, progress, principal, and threshold evidence.

The public terminal may narrate these transitions, but the narration is always labeled `RULE`; the underlying Pons and Robinhood Chain facts remain labeled `CHAIN` or `CONNECTED` evidence.

## Eight-arm Divergence

At material phases (`ATTACHED`, `ASCENDING`, `GRADUATED`, and `ADAPTING`), ARCHIVE preserves eight deterministic positions around the same evidence. These positions intentionally keep epistemic limits visible: a verified graduation can satisfy DEPTH while CURRENT withholds a momentum claim, CHORUS withholds a holder-distribution claim, AUDITOR refuses to treat status as a safety certificate, and HUNTER refuses to infer intent.

The terminal must disclose that these are rule-layer perspectives recorded at a lifecycle transition. They are not represented as eight simultaneous LLM calls. Individual AI voices remain invoked only when a visitor uses ASK.

## Graduation Ceremony

The ceremony is a chain-triggered interface state, not a scheduled animation. It can open only after the registered target reports verified Pons graduation. It presents the retained phase, pool, observed block, threshold, and target identity, and explicitly states that graduation is a route transition rather than proof of future price, safety, or liquidity quality.

## Verified Micro-milestones

The lifecycle is enriched by smaller evidence-driven memories:

- Curve checkpoints at `10%`, `25%`, `50%`, `75%`, `90%`, and `95%`.
- New retained curve highs and curve retreats of at least five percentage points.
- First target-scoped transaction, first verified pool Swap, and first sampled non-pool participant.
- Verified silence only after fifteen minutes without target activity while Railway and chain freshness remain healthy.
- Observation gaps are recorded separately and suspend silence inference.
- After graduation: verified pool, first external-pool Swap, and one hundred blocks beyond the first retained graduated block.
- A 100-sample post-graduation baseline unlocks `ADAPTING`; later activity may create clearly labeled rule-layer deviation records.

Micro-milestones are deduplicated by target and event key. Repeatable incidents such as silence, recovery, observer gaps, curve retreats, and behavioral deviations use explicit sequences or block evidence rather than decorative counters.

## Brand Personality

Scientific, intelligent, restrained. O8 speaks with calm precision. Individual arms may be excited, suspicious, anxious, curious, protective, or uncertain, but emotion affects expression rather than facts.

## Anti-references

- A mascot with an unrelated chatbot attached.
- Eight identical model responses with different names or avatars.
- Unverifiable claims of consciousness, autonomy, profit, or prediction.
- Marketing landing pages, feature grids, long scrolling narratives, and generic AI copy.
- Neon cyberpunk, glossy science fiction, glass panels, gradients, and cartoon animals.
- Fake chain activity, unexplained numerical precision, or synthetic content labeled as external fact.

## Design Principles

1. The octopus is the AI architecture: eight arms are eight specialized agents, not decoration.
2. Chain facts are immutable inputs; personality changes interpretation and language, never evidence.
3. Disagreement is a product feature. ARM-08 preserves dissent instead of flattening it into false consensus.
4. Every factual answer cites blocks, transactions, contracts, wallets, sources, or retained event IDs.
5. Deep-sea language remains a visual metaphor; Robinhood Chain and AI are the actual system.
6. The single-viewport terminal is an instrument inside the story world, not a page explaining it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast where practical. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive layout. Agent identity must never depend on color alone; every response includes an arm number, name, role, and textual mood.
